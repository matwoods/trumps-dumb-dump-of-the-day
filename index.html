Audit Protocol

The 24-Hour Automated Schedule

1. 24h News & Speech Ingestion

Every morning at 08:00 AM UTC, the scheduled cloud server gathers recent campaign rally logs, press releases, and Truth Social posts from the past 24 hours.

2. Mandatory 3-Source Fact-Check

No statement is published without verification across 3 distinct sources: (1) Primary transcripts/video, (2) Certified fact-checkers (PolitiFact, FactCheck.org, AP), and (3) Corroborating public records or corporate filings.

3. Balanced Media Framing Analysis

The pipeline contrasts how critical outlets (NPR, NYT, MSNBC) critique the statement against how conservative/official sources (Fox News, Sky News, campaign press releases) defend it.

4. Automatic Cloud Publish

GitHub Actions commits the updated data to data/quotes.json and rebuilds the site with zero downtime.

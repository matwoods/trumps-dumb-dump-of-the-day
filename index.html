RECORD #2026-10-10 October 9, 2026 • Oncenter Rally, Syracuse, NY
REFUTED BY EVIDENCE • SCORE: 9.4/10
Primary Verbatim Transcription
"I brought Micron here with my tariffs. If it wasn't for my tariffs, they'd be building in China. We made the $100 billion deal happen right here in Syracuse. Nobody else could have made this deal."

Delivered in Syracuse, NY during an 80-minute campaign rally in support of Bruce Blakeman.

Executive Determination of Accuracy Categorically False • 9.4 / 10

Blatantly false. Micron committed the $100B investment four years earlier in 2022 under the bipartisan CHIPS and Science Act, which Trump previously condemned and called a 'horrible bill' that should be repealed.

1

Announced in 2022, Not 2026

Micron publicly finalized its commitment to invest up to $100B in Clay, NY on October 4, 2022 — four full years prior to this rally speech.

2

Incentivized by the CHIPS Act, Not Tariffs

Micron CEO Sanjay Mehrotra explicitly cited federal grants from the 2022 CHIPS and Science Act and NY Green CHIPS tax incentives as the determining factors. Tariffs played zero role in the agreement.

3

Trump Campaign Previously Vowed to Repeal the Law

Trump repeatedly condemned the CHIPS Act as a "horrible bill" and suggested repealing it. Taking personal credit for a manufacturing campus made possible by legislation he opposed is contradicted by the public record.

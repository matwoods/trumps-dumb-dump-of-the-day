The Verbatim Statement

"I brought Micron here with my tariffs. If it wasn't for my tariffs, they'd be building in China. We made the $100 billion deal happen right here in Syracuse. Nobody else could have made this deal."

Delivered during an 80-minute speech at the Oncenter in Syracuse, NY.

Fact-Check Absurdity Index 9.4 / 10 • Extremely Detached from Reality

Blatantly false. Micron committed the $100B investment four years earlier in 2022 under the bipartisan CHIPS Act, which Trump previously condemned and called a 'horrible bill'.

01

Announced in 2022, Not 2026

Micron publicly finalized its commitment to invest up to $100B in Clay, NY on October 4, 2022 — four years prior to this rally speech.

02

Incentivized by the CHIPS Act, Not Tariffs

Micron CEO Sanjay Mehrotra explicitly cited the federal bipartisan CHIPS and Science Act of 2022 and NY Green CHIPS tax credits as the determining factors. Tariffs played zero role in the agreement.

03

Trump Repeatedly Attacked the Funding Law

Trump previously called the CHIPS Act "horrible" and "a waste of billions of dollars," calling for its repeal. Taking credit for a plant made possible by a law he campaigned against is peak irony.
